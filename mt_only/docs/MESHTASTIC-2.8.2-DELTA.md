# Meshtastic 2.8.2 differences from the XIAO qualification baseline

The preserved baseline qualifies Meshtastic `v2.8.1.8e6a88d`. This source comparison ends at the November 4 prerelease `v2.8.2.d5f1619`. The two tags differ by 126 commits and 341 files. The firmware's protobuf pin advanced from `8f97d66a` to `8d0ed0c1`. Firmware, Android updater and bootloader sources have separate version histories. [Upstream comparison](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/compare/v2.8.1.8e6a88d...v2.8.2.d5f1619) · [2.8.2 prerelease](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/releases/tag/v2.8.2.d5f1619)

The evidence column below records what had been tested on the preserved 2.8.1 baseline. The separate port has its own [2.8.2 source preview](../../README.md).

## Module inventory

| Both upstream source trees | 2.8.1 | 2.8.2 | Additions and applicability |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Distinct constructors in `Modules.cpp` | 42 | 44 | `DMShellModule` is conditional on Portduino and its shell option. `DMShellTestModule` requires a special test-peer build. Neither is a normal XIAO companion module. |
| Module C++ files, excluding sensor backends and registration | 44 | 46 | The two shell implementation files account for the increase. |
| Sensor backend C++ files | 46 | 48 | RAKSensorHub and its uplink decoder serve the RAK2560 WisMesh Hub. This hardware is absent from the bare XIAO. |

These counts use the same constructor and file rules as the 2.8.1 inventory. None of the existing entries disappeared. Changes inside existing modules account for most XIAO-relevant differences. ## Changes that affect an XIAO comparison

| Area | What changed upstream in 2.8.2 | What the present ZT results establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MeshBeacon | Each target can pin its frequency slot. Offers can advertise a slot the receiver could not derive. Target selection now uses the indicated channel index and validates the region, preset and slot together. | The 2.8.1 beacon tests cover their original target fields. New-field persistence, wire encoding, selection and rejection require a 2.8.2 oracle. [MeshBeacon change](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12126) |
| Signing and key verification | Upstream revised Compatible, Balanced and Strict behavior, including signing on AES-CCM channels and unsigned identity updates. Key verification now refuses licensed-mode requests and can create a newly confirmed responder's NodeDB entry. | ZT tests its existing signing, owner pinning and key-verification rules. The 198-contact experiment measures capacity under those rules. A 2.8.2 comparison needs independent transmit, receive and identity cases. [Signing policy](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12191) · [Licensed mode](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12201) · [Responder commit](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12194) |
| Relaying | NodeInfo and NeighborInfo relays preserve the incoming payload. Traffic Management removed its in-transit Position precision clamp. The router handles known echoes first and then drops other inbound packets bearing its own node number. | ZT preserves authenticated payload bytes. Its 2.8.1 port can update an eligible unsigned licensed NeighborInfo, and the selected Position policy suppresses an over-precise relay. Own-number filtering across all module and phone deliveries still needs a 2.8.2 comparison. [NodeInfo](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12088) · [NeighborInfo](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12090) · [Position](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12039) · [Router](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12270) |
| Position deduplication | New Traffic Management configurations default to a six-hour window. The position floor derives from that window. Previously saved values and existing role caps carry forward. | Upstream's first-boot and previously configured cases would need separate tests. [Traffic Management change](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/11765) |
| NodeDB, MQTT and Store & Forward | Upstream preserves deferred saves, encodes satellite maps in place, publishes eligible opaque PKI transit packets to MQTT, and fixes first-time Store & Forward enablement. | ZT has its own persistence and MQTT admission paths. Its stock 2.8.1 Store & Forward role tests and 198 public/private contact tests do not qualify the full set of newer behaviors. [Deferred saves](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/11930) · [Satellite maps](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12293) · [MQTT](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12110) · [First enable](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12168) |
| Radio timing and USB | Upstream changed listen-before-talk and CAD timing, interrupted RX handling, staging TX after modem changes, and recovery of a latched ESP32-S3 hardware CDC transmit path. ZT uses Zephyr drivers and a different USB implementation. | Stock updater and USB tests establish the named transports on this board. Modem timing and USB stall recovery need their own comparison. The 2.8.2 BLE DFU reset change applies to nRF52. [Channel admission](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/11137) · [SX126x TX](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12101) · [S3 USB](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12076) · [Nordic DFU](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/pull/12220) |

The release also changes Portduino Bluetooth and notifications, optional displays and games, board-specific GPS and radio support, and CI. Some require hardware beyond the bare XIAO. The later `d5f1619` tag adds the in-place NodeDB encoding and a release-workflow change atop the earlier `v2.8.2.4c89b69` prerelease. [Changes between the two 2.8.2 tags](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/compare/v2.8.2.4c89b69...v2.8.2.d5f1619)

For the 2.8.2 branch, the stock registration owners `DMShellModule` and `DMShellTestModule` live in `DMShell` and `DMShellTestModule`. The former requires Portduino shell support and the latter a dedicated test-peer build. The `RAKSensorHub` and `RAKSensorHubUplink` backends depend on RAK2560 hardware. Neither board profile enables those four additions.
