# Stock sensor channels

The T096 and XIAO release overlays describe the hardware on the stock boards. Accessory overlays can declare these additional device-tree aliases.

| Alias | Measurement |
| --- | --- |
| `zt-environment`, `zt-environment-2` | Zephyr temperature, humidity, pressure, gas resistance, calibrated light, IR, distance, voltage and current |
| `air-quality`, `air-quality-2`, `air-quality-3` | Plantower standard/atmospheric mass and particle counts, CO₂ and the accompanying temperature and humidity |
| `zt-optical` | MAX30102 with paired red/IR FIFO data, 100 Hz, 4-sample averaging, 411 µs pulse width, 4096 nA range and LED amplitudes of 60. |
| `body-temperature` | A dedicated contact thermometer with a measured Celsius channel. Use `health-sensor` for optical results and driver-specific object-temperature channels. |
| `zt-one-wire-0` through `zt-one-wire-7` | DS2482-800 channel temperature probes. USB `sensor_config.ds248x_config.main_temperature_channel` selects the primary ambient reading. |
| `zt-shtxx` | SHT3x/SHT4x repeatability. Point an environment alias at the same device for temperature and humidity. |
| `zt-scd4x` | SCD40/SCD41 local USB calibration. Point an air-quality alias at the same device for its periodic measurement. |

The SCD40/SCD41 starts in the operating mode selected by its device tree. USB `sensor_config.scd4x_config` supports all seven fields in pinned Meshtastic. Setting `set_asc=true` enables automatic calibration and applies an optional CO₂ baseline in ppm. Setting `set_asc=false` with a target triggers forced recalibration and restores the prior ASC setting if recalibration fails. The sensor must have measured stable reference air for its specified warm-up period. Altitude takes precedence when a request also supplies pressure. Temperature-offset calibration requires a fresh sample. Factory reset clears the sensor's stored settings and calibration history. A power-mode request selects normal or low-power periodic acquisition until reboot, including for a device initially configured in single-shot mode.

The SCD4x worker reports its driver result to USB after completion. Replaying its last request ID and body in the same session reuses the outcome, including a failure. A fresh request ID retries a failed operation. The driver waits 500 ms after stopping measurements, 1 ms after writing the ASC target and 800 ms for EEPROM persistence. Board profiles enable the worker only when the `zt-scd4x` accessory is present. SCD41 exposes the initial and standard ASC periods in hours through its native driver attributes. The pinned Meshtastic schema provides no wire fields for either period. Changing the baseline or operating mode preserves them. [Stock SCD4x handler](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/blob/8e6a88d06f44cad26f1e8d7cd402939ccacb9b4c/src/modules/Telemetry/Sensor/SCD4XSensor.cpp) · [Sensirion command source](https://github.com/Sensirion/arduino-i2c-scd4x/blob/1.1.0/src/SensirionI2cScd4x.cpp)

USB `sensor_config.shtxx_config.set_accuracy` selects low (0), medium (1) or high (2) repeatability. SHT3x and SHT4x use the selected command and conversion wait on their next single-shot reading. A periodic SHT3x keeps its measurement rate while changing repeatability. A failed periodic restart attempts to restore its prior configuration. Invalid levels and driver failures return a Routing error. [Stock SHTxx handler](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware/blob/8e6a88d06f44cad26f1e8d7cd402939ccacb9b4c/src/modules/Telemetry/Sensor/SHTXXSensor.cpp)

The additional I²C accessory providers use their own declared aliases. One worker serializes local USB requests, acquisition and driver waits. Calibration writes and reads stay on the selected bus address. A missing sensor, malformed response, checksum failure, timeout or failed persistence yields an error. Both release boards can omit the entire worker.

| Alias | Commands and reported measurements |
| --- | --- |
| `zt-scd30` | ASC, forced calibration, sensor altitude, reference temperature, measurement interval and soft reset. A reference of 100 clears the temperature offset. Fresh CO₂, temperature and humidity come from CRC-checked floating-point readings. |
| `zt-sen5x` | One-shot or continuous operation, manual and weekly fan cleaning and particulate measurements. SEN54 and SEN55 also accept the schema’s temperature-reference field through Sensirion’s three-word offset command. SEN50 lacks the temperature channel. |
| `zt-sen6x` | One-shot or continuous operation, manual and weekly fan cleaning, temperature offset and model-specific PM/gas readings. CO₂-capable SEN63C, SEN66 and SEN69C also support ASC, forced calibration with rollback, altitude, ambient pressure and CO₂ factory reset. |
| `zt-as3935` | Antenna tuning capacitance of 0–120 pF in 8 pF increments, with register readback and persistent trim. Polled interrupts feed the one-hour strike count, last available distance and an immediate environment broadcast request. |
| `zt-nau7802` | USB `set_scale=0` tares from 64 conversions. A known nonzero mass in kg calibrates the scale from another 64. Weight reports use a measured average and a saved nonzero factor. Conversion timeouts preserve the prior calibration. |

The nine SEN models are identified by product name and serial number. With a valid UTC later than January 1, 2018, the first reading sets the maintenance baseline. After strictly more than seven days, the accessory worker starts fan cleaning. It records the new baseline after the 10.5-second cycle completes. Automatic cleaning retries a failed start after one minute.

On SEN54, SEN55, SEN65, SEN66, SEN68 and SEN69C, the worker checkpoints the four-word VOC algorithm state after an hour of uninterrupted gas measurement, then at five-minute intervals. It verifies I²C word CRCs and stores a versioned record with the model, serial, UTC and a checksum. Before starting the gas engine it can restore a matching, nonzero snapshot less than ten minutes old. A sensor that boots before the clock is set rechecks the snapshot when time becomes available. A future capture date, changed sensor or corrupted record causes fresh learning. The settings store verifies writes by readback and retries failed maintenance writes. The factory preference reset erases both families’ history plus measurement modes, antenna trim and scale calibration.

A measured AS3935 lightning strike can advance the next mesh environment report for up to five minutes. The normal channel, airtime and queue admission checks still apply. Private request replies and phone-only updates preserve the pending event. Queue acceptance consumes it once. Periodic reports retain their configured interval and phone copies retain their own cadence. Physical qualification requires the matching accessory, including a known scale reference mass and a tuned lightning antenna. The same worker supports the explicitly declared `zt-radsens` and `zt-rcwl9620` I²C devices. RadSens reads the 500-second static intensity register in µR/h. RCWL9620 waits 100 ms on the worker thread and reports millimetres up to 4,500. The pinned donor conversion bodies run against identical register data, including zero and saturation. A failed transaction omits the affected reading. Successful measurements are available to both periodic telemetry and phone requests.

A declared `zt-ltr390` alternates 20-bit UV and ambient cycles on the accessory worker. A ready sample updates one half of the pair and retains the preceding other half as stock does. The phone protobuf uses the upstream `uv_lux` field for the UV index. The sensor suite compiles the donor’s paired readout and checks each result and gain change. [Adafruit LTR390 1.1.2](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_LTR390/tree/cd1d7175408d5ed5e928cc200d65de842a1d3317)

The `zt-lark` weather station supplies temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed and direction through its five stock commands. Dropzone can use the first four readings as soon as direction is available. When only Dropzone asks for the station, the worker skips the humidity command. A humidity failure leaves the measured temperature, pressure and wind available to telemetry. A Dropzone module targeting the same bus and address requests its weather from the same worker. This preserves command ordering and lets Dropzone request a reading with periodic weather disabled. The worker compares the resulting metrics with the pinned donor method. The optional `zt-rainfall` SEN0575 verifies the vendor and product ID before selecting its one-hour and 24-hour windows. Both rain counts travel in millimetres through periodic and requested telemetry. [DFRobot Lark driver](https://github.com/meshtastic/DFRobot_LarkWeatherStation/tree/4de3a9cadef0f6a5220a8a906cf9775b02b0040d) · [DFRobot rainfall driver](https://github.com/DFRobot/DFRobot_RainfallSensor/tree/38fea5e02b40a5430be6dab39a99a6f6347d667e)

The independent `accessories.overlay` fixture declares each of these chips on a 100 kHz bus. The sensor test drives the actual worker with simulated I²C peripherals and clocks, including USB request replay, storage failure, absent or incompatible sensor responses and persisted calibration readback.

Set `CONFIG_MT_STOCK_ENVIRONMENT_CHANNELS=y` to include legacy INA volts and milliamps in environment reports on a board with INA monitors alone. Either named environment alias enables these fields automatically. Additional power monitors retain their native order across read failures. One ampere is 1,000 milliamperes, one meter is 1,000 millimeters and the pinned BME680 firmware reports kiloohms. A generic VOC channel reports ppb and supplies no dimensionless VOC index.

The included `scd41.overlay` is a build fixture for the Zephyr SCD41, SHT4x, DS2482/DS18B20 and MAX30102 drivers on I2C0. It also checks that disabled accessory aliases do not link a device.

The stock health algorithm comes from SparkFun MAX3010x v1.1.2, commit `72d5308df500ae1a64cc9d63e950c68c96dc78d5` (Maxim MAXREFDES117). The port uses the 100-sample red/IR window, lookup table and reference validity outputs. It bounds the end of a plateau and widens intermediate products for 18-bit samples. Invalid results are omitted. The health temperature field uses the declared thermometer. Optical contact and accuracy require a physical test.

Run `python3 mt_only/tests/stock_sensor_channels/run.py --stock <meshtastic-checkout> --maxim <sparkfun-checkout>` to compare against the pinned donors, exercise both enum ABIs, and drive public packets and sensor Admin through the normal firmware handlers. The suite also compiles the locked SCD4x and SHT C drivers against deterministic I²C and time fixtures. These tests cover frames, CRC, timing, failures and retries. Physical sensor response and accuracy require accessory testing.

An external ADS1115 or ADS1015 can produce eight ADC voltage fields through the same environment worker. Declare `zt-ads1x15` at 0x48 for channels 0–3 and/or `zt-ads1x15-alt` at 0x49, 0x4a or 0x4b for channels 4–7. Use `zephtastic,ads1115` or `zephtastic,ads1015` on a 100 kHz bus. These bindings give the worker sole ownership of the converter registers. See `../stock_ads1x15/ads.overlay` for a dual-device build fixture. Each device contributes four fresh channels after all four conversions succeed. A NACK or conversion timeout suppresses its group.

The pinned Meshtastic code applies the same unsigned gain thresholds and 32,768 divisor to the ADS1015 and ADS1115, including the ADS1015's sign-extended 12-bit result. ZT preserves the stock values. Run `python3 mt_only/tests/stock_ads1x15/run.py --stock <meshtastic-checkout> --adafruit <Adafruit_ADS1X15-checkout>` to compare both stock ADC types, register traffic and all eight encoded fields against Meshtastic and Adafruit ADS1X15 v2.6.2 on both enum ABIs.

The optional `zt-sfa30` alias at 0x5d selects the Sensirion formaldehyde sensor on a declared 100 kHz bus. It publishes formaldehyde in ppb, relative humidity in percent and temperature in °C in AirQualityMetrics after the stock ten-second warm-up. All three sensor words have a CRC. The continuous measurement stops when air telemetry is disabled.

The optional `zt-rak12035` alias at 0x20 publishes soil moisture and temperature in EnvironmentMetrics. Stock enables this probe on the RAK4631. On other boards the binding requires an externally powered probe with its own active-low reset line. The worker wakes that probe, verifies its calibration, reads both values, and sends it back to sleep. Firmware versions 1 and 2 calculate percent from calibrated capacitance. Later versions supply moisture directly. The worker samples once per minute and expires a reading 90 seconds after acquisition.

See the build-only overlay in `mt_only/tests/stock_air_soil/accessories.overlay`. Run `python3 mt_only/tests/stock_air_soil/run.py --stock <meshtastic> --sfa <Sensirion-arduino-i2c-sfa3x> --core <Sensirion-arduino-core> --rak <RAK12035_SoilMoisture>` for the pinned driver and stock handler comparisons.

The optional HM330x producer accepts a `seeed,hm330x` on an existing `air-quality`, `air-quality-2` or `air-quality-3` alias, or a dedicated `zt-hm330x` alias at 0x40. For a worker-only declaration use `zephtastic,hm330x` on a 400 kHz bus. It waits 30 seconds after selecting I²C, verifies the 29-byte frame checksum and emits the stock standard PM1, PM2.5 and PM10 mass fields. The atmospheric byte offsets represent separate measurements. The fixture `mt_only/tests/stock_pm/hm330x.overlay` exercises the existing alias. Run `python3 mt_only/tests/stock_pm/run.py --stock <meshtastic> --hm330x <meshtastic/Seeed_PM2_5_sensor_HM3301>` to compare the two producers against the pinned sources.

The PMSA003I producer accepts the native `plantower,pmsa003i` with a normal air-quality alias, or `zephtastic,pmsa003i` on `zt-pmsa003i` at 0x12 on a 100 kHz bus. It requests the 32-byte Plantower frame directly, checks the header and checksum, and emits all three standard masses, three atmospheric masses and six particle counts per 0.1 litre. With an active-high `set-gpios` pin, the existing air telemetry scheduler wakes the device before its report deadline and gives it the full stock 30-second warm-up. It sleeps between longer intervals. A probe with SET tied high supplies samples immediately. The host suite exercises both hardware configurations, both enum ABIs, power transitions, failure paths and the encoded periodic and solicited reports.
